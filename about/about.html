Biography


I have always loved to draw and use creativity as a way to express myself. Over time, I realized that I wanted to use those creative skills to help other people and bring their ideas to life. This led me toward graphic design and eventually to studying Visual Communications. As a designer, I am especially interested in graphic design, branding and identity, digital media, and illustration. I enjoy exploring how visuals can communicate an idea, feeling, or message while still being visually engaging. I am interested in the process of taking an idea and turning it into something that people can see, understand, and connect with. In the future, I see myself working as part of a creative team. I would love to collaborate with other designers and creatives on a variety of projects, whether that means designing for a pop-up event, creating merchandise for an artist/company, or helping a company develop and strengthen its brand identity. I want my career to allow me to continue learning, experimenting, and growing as a designer while using my creativity to help others communicate their ideas. Some extra things about me is that I am a big comic reader, and listen to music almost all day everyday. I love drawing, cosplay, doing small projects with friends, cooking, and playing video games like Rivals, Overwatch, Resident Evil, Phasmophobia, REPO, and more! When I am not inside, I like to run, explore trails, and find new places to eat or take photographs of in the city.
