Process Reflection

Unlike most in this class I have zero coding experience whatsoever. I do have knowledge of hierarchy in design and for this project I was a little inspired by the “Geronimo Stilton” books. The hierarchy I used helped change the tone by making certain words, phrases, and names stand out more than others. Size, bold, italics, or separated from the rest, I tried creating a sort of humorous tone, the kind of tone you would have reading a book to a small child. With more time and more knowledge I am satisfied not only because I feel it makes the the story more interactive and exciting than a traditional book but also because I did it myself with no previous knowledge of HTML.
