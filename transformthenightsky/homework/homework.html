Process Reflection

For this assignment, I experimented with different css transforms to make the stars interactive. I used scale(), rotate(), skew(), and translate() to create different hover effects. Combining multiple transforms made the stars more interesting and the transition-duration property made the movements smoother. I also experimented with the absolute positioning to spread the stars across the screen. One challenge was asjusting their placement and makiung sure everything was displayed correctly. This project was cool to work on to see how different transforms affect the object and will be looking forward to applying it in different elements of my website. But, the thing that this assignment also did was make me feel a bit more confident in my positioning of objects as I didn't struggle as much as I did in other assignments.
